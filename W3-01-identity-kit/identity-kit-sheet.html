Style note

Standing instruction in my Claude Project, so every build inherits it.

Fonts: Space Grotesk for headings, IBM Plex Sans for body. Colors: paper #FAF9F7, ink #16181D, main #2E4B6B, accent #C2410C, dark mode variants in tokens.css. Accent is reserved for the single call to action.

Mood: a lab notebook, not a brochure. Plain, quiet, and slightly technical, so the numbers and the writing are the loudest thing on the page. Generous white space, one column, no gradients, no shadows, no animation.

My claim is that my numbers hold up when someone checks them. A page with gradients and motion undercuts that before a reader reaches a sentence, because it is visibly trying to impress. Quiet is the same claim in another medium.
